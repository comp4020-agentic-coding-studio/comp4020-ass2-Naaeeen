Use this packet for [Week 9](/sessions/09-ordinal-scale/). All three cards refer
to sections of one official English film page, not three independent accounts.
The required text takes about seven minutes. No film purchase, subscription or
AR device is needed.

## What kind of source is this

The publisher's [Ordinal Scale story page](https://sao-movie.net/us/story/story.html)
introduces the film, its device and its game to an audience. It is promotional
material about the story, not an independent technical evaluation or an in-world
witness statement. The page does not provide a publication date; its copyright
line should not be substituted for one.

In our course's fictional 2035 classroom, we study these events historically.
The screen adaptations and their publicity remain the actual sources we can
check. A teaching proposal must therefore remain visibly separate from an event
claimed to have occurred.

## A. From Full-Dive to Augma

**Read:** **Prologue**, the first section of the
[official story page](https://sao-movie.net/us/story/story.html).
About two minutes.

**Teaching paraphrase:** The introduction places NerveGear's Full-Dive in 2022
and Augma's arrival in 2026. Augma uses AR rather than Full-Dive, allowing play
while awake. Asuna's group is already playing when Kirito joins.

**Use it for:** comparing how the introduction presents entry into the two
systems. Its promise of safety is a promotional claim; this section does not
supply a safety evaluation. Keep the story dates separate from publication dates.

**Citation label:** Ordinal Scale A, "Prologue".

## B. An interface worn in the world

**Read:** **AR (Augmented Reality) Type Information Terminal “Augma”**, the
second section of the [same page](https://sao-movie.net/us/story/story.html).
About two minutes.

**Teaching paraphrase:** Augma is a small wearable device. The description says
it delivers visual, sound and touch information while its user remains awake,
and connects these functions to game-like fitness and health activities.

**Use it for:** identifying which bodily relationship the description promises.
The text does not establish how every body can use the device or which access
barriers its designers considered. Those are questions to investigate.

**Citation label:** Ordinal Scale B, "AR … Augma" section.

## C. Place and rank

**Read:** **“Ordinal Scale”**, the final section of the
[story page](https://sao-movie.net/us/story/story.html). About three minutes.

**Teaching paraphrase:** Players improve their rank through activities including
collecting items at physical locations and defeating monsters. Rank determines
status, and higher ranks confer substantial advantages in player-versus-player
contests.

**Use it for:** separating what the interface lets players do from how the game
allocates advantage. A proposed fairer system would be our design response,
not a feature this source says the game already provides.

**Citation label:** Ordinal Scale C, "Ordinal Scale" section.

## Terms and boundaries

**AR** means augmented reality; this page contrasts it with Full-Dive.
**PvP** means player versus player. **Rank** describes an ordered position;
card C explains how that position matters in this particular game.

The seminar's shared-square example is invented. The page does not establish
a particular public-space incident, an accessibility policy or a memory-extraction
mechanism. Claims about those matters need additional identifiable sources.

If the page is unavailable, these labelled paraphrases contain enough information
for the interface comparison. Mark your notes "paraphrase only". They are
original teaching summaries, not quotations or a transcript; consult the original
before quoting its language in the [exhibition](/assessments/digital-history-exhibition/).
