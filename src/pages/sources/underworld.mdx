For [Week 10](/sessions/10-artificial-life/), read A–C and E alongside the
[lecture](/lectures/week-10/). For [Week 11](/sessions/11-protecting-underworld/),
focus on D and return to E. Card E looks ahead to the aftermath; Week 11 returns
to the conflict in D. No episode purchase or prior knowledge of the full
arc is required. The cards below provide an English route into each activity.

## Orient yourself

Underworld is the virtual setting of *Alicization*. Eugeo and Alice are central
to the selected accounts of its inhabitants. **NPC** means non-player character;
**AGI** means artificial general intelligence, the term used for Alice in E.
The **Human Empire** is one of the societies named in these sources. **ALO**
is the earlier virtual world from which D describes volunteers joining the conflict.

These official publisher summaries and profiles introduce screen stories to an
audience. They are not independent eyewitnesses, experimental reports or complete
institutional records. Compare what they foreground without treating repeated
publisher claims as independent confirmation.

## A. Meeting Eugeo

**What and where:** Aniplex of America, press release dated **23 August 2018**;
read only **STORY on page 2** of the
[four-page English PDF](https://aniplexusa.com/pdf/PR_082318_SAOAlicization.pdf).
Stop before STAFF. About three minutes; skip the premiere and ticket information.
The date identifies publication, not an Underworld event.

**Teaching paraphrase:** Kirito enters an unfamiliar virtual world, meets Eugeo
and tries to understand his situation. Eugeo is presented as an apparent NPC
with human-like emotions. Kirito recalls an unexplained childhood connection
with Eugeo and Alice.

**Use and limit:** the juxtaposition makes classification and remembered
relationships useful questions. A promotional story introduction is not a test
establishing consciousness.

**Citation label:** Underworld A, p. 2, "STORY".

## B. Operation and authority

**What and where:** the official English
[Alicization character page](https://sao-alicization.com/1st/character/).
Read only the **Cardinal** and **Administrator** descriptions, using your
browser's find function if needed. About two minutes. The page is undated.

**Teaching paraphrase:** Cardinal is described as an autonomous program that
operates and controls Underworld. Administrator is presented as the highest
Axiom Church officer and ruler of the Human Empire's people.

**Use and limit:** distinguish a description of operation from one of rule.
These brief profiles do not establish how residents consent to either arrangement
or specify every mechanism through which power is exercised.

**Citation label:** Underworld B, "Cardinal" or "Administrator" profile.

## C. Punishment and disrupted memory

**What and where:** **Episode 11, “Central Cathedral,” STORY** on the
[official English episode page](https://sao-alicization.com/1st/story/11.html).
About two minutes; no video required. The page gives no publication date.

**Teaching paraphrase:** Kirito and Eugeo are treated as offenders after violating
the Taboo Index. Alice appears as an Integrity Knight but seems not to remember
their childhood relationship. The boys are imprisoned in the Central Cathedral.

**Use and limit:** ask how a rule and an apparently disrupted relationship affect
recognition. The synopsis does not explain the full cause of Alice's memory
condition or a complete mechanism of obedience.

**Citation label:** Underworld C, episode 11, "Central Cathedral", STORY.

## D. Outside players enter the conflict

**What and where:** **INTRODUCTION, Part 2** on the official English
[War of Underworld introduction](https://sao-alicization.com/intro/).
About four minutes. Select Part 2 if a different tab opens; locate the paragraph
beginning "The final load test". The page is undated.

**Teaching paraphrase:** Outside players are recruited into the conflict. Asuna
and allies use powerful accounts, and Asuna fights alongside Human Empire
forces. ALO players also join despite risks. The introduction presents
Underworld's continued existence and artificial intelligence as stakes of the war.

**Use and limit:** examine unequal powers and different reasons for entering.
The text does not give a complete account of residents' agreement to outside
intervention. Do not turn that gap into a claim that all residents agreed or refused.

**Citation label:** Underworld D, "Introduction", Part 2.

## E. Alice before an audience

**What and where:** **Episode 22, “Alice,” STORY** on the official English
[War of Underworld episode page](https://sao-alicization.com/story/?id=ep22).
About two minutes. This is episode 22 of *War of Underworld*, not the earlier
*Alicization* episode list. The page is undated.

**Teaching paraphrase:** After the Rath attack, Rinko introduces Alice publicly
as artificial general intelligence. Alice responds to questions from reporters.

**Use and limit:** the synopsis identifies a public introduction and an exchange
with an audience. It does not establish citizenship, a legal-rights settlement
or a general scientific criterion for personhood.

**Citation label:** Underworld E, episode 22, "Alice", STORY.

## When a link fails

The labelled paraphrases provide the facts needed for the seminar tasks. Mark
work based on them "paraphrase only" and identify the card. They are original
teaching summaries, not transcripts. The PDF's STORY section contains selectable
text for a reader or plain-text document; the other originals are English HTML.
Check the original before quoting, and record an unresolved access problem in
your source notes.

## What we add in class

The Week 10 archive hearing and Week 11 stakeholder council are invented teaching
scenarios. Their proposed recognition criteria, emergency examples and intervention
conditions are arguments to test, not recovered SAO documents. Do not invent
character testimony to strengthen a position. Carry that distinction into the
[Digital history exhibition](/assessments/digital-history-exhibition/).
