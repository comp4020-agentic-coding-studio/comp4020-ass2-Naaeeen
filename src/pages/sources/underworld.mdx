## Orient yourself

**Underworld** was a virtual world whose inhabitants became central to questions
about artificial life. **Kirito** was a visitor; **Eugeo** and **Alice** were
among the people whose lives he encountered there. The **Human Empire** and
**Dark Territory** were opposing powers within that world.

**NPC** means non-player character, a category used for Eugeo in account A.
**AGI** means artificial general intelligence, the term used at Alice's public
introduction in E. Neither label alone answers every question about someone's
experience, relationships or standing.

## A. Meeting Eugeo

<p id="a1"><strong>A1.</strong> Kirito found himself in an unfamiliar virtual world with only a hazy recollection of what had happened before he entered. While looking for an explanation and a way to leave, he met Eugeo, an inhabitant of that world.</p>

<p id="a2"><strong>A2.</strong> Eugeo appeared to be an NPC, yet showed emotions comparable to a human's. As Kirito formed a bond with him, he recalled an unexplained childhood connection involving Eugeo and a girl called Alice. The remembered relationship complicated his understanding of the place and its people.</p>

**Question:** what does a category explain, and what does it leave open? An
account of emotions and relationships is not a complete test of consciousness.

## B. Operation and authority

<p id="b1"><strong>B1.</strong> Cardinal was an autonomous program operating and controlling Underworld. She had withdrawn to the great library inside the Central Cathedral.</p>

<p id="b2"><strong>B2.</strong> Administrator was the highest officer of the Axiom Church and ruled the people of the Human Empire. Describing who operated the world and who ruled its people raises separate questions about technical capacity and institutional power.</p>

**Question:** what would establish the legitimacy of a decision, beyond the
ability to carry it out? B does not give a complete account of residents'
agreement to these arrangements.

## C. Punishment and disrupted memory

<p id="c1"><strong>C1.</strong> Kirito and Eugeo were treated as criminals after violating the Taboo Index, the rules named in the case against them. They were imprisoned beneath the Central Cathedral.</p>

<p id="c2"><strong>C2.</strong> Alice appeared as an Integrity Knight involved in their arrest. Kirito and Eugeo recognised their childhood friend, but she seemed not to remember the time they had spent together. This account records that apparent absence without explaining its full cause.</p>

**Question:** what happens to recognition when remembered relationships no
longer match? Do not invent a complete mechanism of memory or obedience to
resolve a gap in the account.

## D. Outside players enter the conflict

<p id="d1"><strong>D1.</strong> War engulfed Underworld. Outside players were recruited to attack the Human Empire, while other visitors joined its defence. The conflict placed the world's continued existence and its artificial inhabitants at stake.</p>

<p id="d2"><strong>D2.</strong> Asuna and her allies entered using exceptionally powerful accounts, and she fought alongside the Human Empire's forces. Players from ALO also joined despite the risks. Visitors therefore arrived with different purposes and unequal capacities to act.</p>

**Question:** how should outside help be authorised and reviewed? D does not
establish that every resident agreed to an intervention, or that every resident
opposed it. Your council's conditions are proposals to evaluate.

## E. Alice before an audience

<p id="e1"><strong>E1.</strong> After the Rath attack, Rinko, the head of a research institution, introduced Alice publicly as artificial general intelligence. The introduction brought an Underworld inhabitant before an audience outside that world.</p>

<p id="e2"><strong>E2.</strong> Alice answered questions from the assembled reporters. That exchange established a public encounter; this account does not establish citizenship, a legal-rights settlement or a general criterion for personhood.</p>

**Question:** distinguish being publicly introduced, being listened to and
having a particular formal status. Which step can this account support?

Everything required for the activities is on this page. Cite the file, account

## What we add in class

The archive hearing and stakeholder council are **classroom proposals**. Their
recognition criteria, emergency examples and conditions for intervention are
arguments to test, not recovered records of meetings. Do not put invented words
into a person's mouth. Keep these proposals distinct in the
[Digital history exhibition](/assessments/digital-history-exhibition/).
