Read only the cards assigned for your seminar. The official English pages
are short and free; no episode purchase or earlier viewing is needed. Each
teaching paraphrase contains enough to begin the relevant classroom activity
if the linked page is unavailable. Label that route in your notes and use
the original before quoting it.

## How to use this packet

These are promotional summaries from the official Sword Art Online USA
website. They introduce selected television stories, not independent
survivor testimony. Their accounts let us compare how problems, relationships
and choices are presented. Keep a reported event separate from an explanation
of its cause, and mark your own proposals as proposals.

The episode pages do not give publication dates. Cite the arc, episode,
heading, URL and access date, using "n.d." if needed. An episode number or
relative time within its story is not a date of publication.

**Names and places:** Kazuto is the person who uses the name Kirito in games;
Asuna is another SAO player. ALO means *Alfheim Online* and GGO means *Gun Gale
Online*. The packets introduce the people needed for each activity as they
appear; you do not need a cast list memorised in advance.

## Week 5: what escape leaves unfinished

### R1. Return is uneven

**Read:** **#15 / Return**, the short synopsis on the
[official Fairy Dance episode 15 page](https://www.swordart-onlineusa.com/fairy_dance/story/?no=15).
Allow three minutes. For our activity, concentrate on the first two sentences
and Kazuto's hospital visit.

**Teaching paraphrase:** Kazuto has returned from SAO, but the synopsis says
300 players, including Asuna, have not regained consciousness. Two months
later he visits her in hospital. The account therefore follows a return
that remains incomplete for some people, while relationships and concerns
continue beyond the end of the game.

**Question to carry:** whose situation disappears if "the players returned"
is treated as a complete account? The activity asks about support and
relationships, not a medical diagnosis.

**Citation location:** R1, episode 15 synopsis, opening three sentences.

### R2. A reason to enter another world

**Read:** **#16 / Land of Fairies** on the
[official Fairy Dance episode 16 page](https://www.swordart-onlineusa.com/fairy_dance/story/?no=16).
Allow three minutes.

**Teaching paraphrase:** Egil shows Kazuto an ALO screenshot containing a girl
who resembles Asuna. A connection between ALO's operator, RECTO, and Sugou
strengthens Kazuto's belief that she is trapped there. He enters as Kirito
and finds his SAO data retained, although the synopsis does not explain
how that retention works.

**Question to carry:** which details motivate a decision, and which
conclusions remain a person's belief at this point in the account?

**Citation location:** R2, episode 16 synopsis. Sugou is introduced in R1
as a person working for Asuna's father; no further corporate history is
needed for the exercise.

## Week 6: a threat under investigation

### R3. What is known at the start?

**Read:** **#01 / The World of Guns** on the
[official Phantom Bullet episode 1 page](https://www.swordart-onlineusa.com/phantom_bullet/story/?no=01),
then the short [official Phantom Bullet introduction](https://www.swordart-onlineusa.com/phantom_bullet/intro/).
Allow five minutes. The introduction's text is presented with its key image;
the paraphrase below provides a text route into the same task.

**Teaching paraphrase:** Kikuoka asks Kirito to investigate deaths reported
in connection with the GGO player called Death Gun. The opening synopsis
links in-game shootings with players found dead, but supplies no causal
mechanism. The introduction describes Kirito's uncertainty about a physical
effect and his entry into GGO, where Sinon helps him.

**Question to carry:** what evidence would distinguish a direct technical
effect from another explanation of the reported connection?

**Citation locations:** R3a, episode 1 synopsis; R3b, Introduction, the
passage about Kikuoka's request and Kirito's response. State which you use.

### R4. Acting before the whole explanation is known

**Read:** **#09 / Death Gun** on the
[official Phantom Bullet episode 9 page](https://www.swordart-onlineusa.com/phantom_bullet/story/?no=09).
Allow three minutes.

**Teaching paraphrase:** During a tournament, Death Gun announces a threat
of death outside the game. Kirito and Sinon decide to work together to
stop him before further casualties. The synopsis presents their protective
cooperation amid the threat; it does not yet give a complete explanation
of how the reported deaths occur.

**Question to carry:** what is the difference between having grounds to take
a threat seriously and having proved a particular cause?

**Citation location:** R4, episode 9 synopsis, the announcement and final
sentence about Kirito and Sinon joining forces.

## Week 7: a world worth participating in

### R5. Making a shared memory

**Read:** **#20 / The Sleeping Knights** on the
[official Mother's Rosario episode 20 page](https://www.swordart-onlineusa.com/mothers_rosario/story/?no=20).
Allow three minutes.

**Teaching paraphrase:** Yuuki asks Asuna to help the Sleeping Knights,
the guild she leads. Its members expect to disband for personal reasons and
want a lasting shared memory. They plan to defeat a floor boss with a
single party, a challenge normally attempted by a much larger combined
group. Asuna hesitates at the difficult request.

**Question to carry:** how does the purpose of an achievement change when
its value lies in doing something together and remembering it?

**Citation location:** R5, episode 20 synopsis, Yuuki's request and its reason.

### R6. Access to school

**Read:** **#23 / The Dream Begins** on the
[official Mother's Rosario episode 23 page](https://www.swordart-onlineusa.com/mothers_rosario/story/?no=23).
Allow three minutes.

**Teaching paraphrase:** Yuuki tests the Medicuboid, a medical-use Full-Dive
device, while staying in hospital. Unable to leave her room, she wants to
experience school again. Asuna asks Kazuto and school friends to help, and
Yuuki takes part in a school day. The account links a technical means of
access with an expressed wish and other people's cooperation.

**Question to carry:** which parts of this participation depend on a device,
and which depend on relationships and decisions? This is the work's
fictional device, not a claim about the effects of real medical equipment.

**Citation location:** R6, episode 23 synopsis, first paragraph before the
visit to Yuuki's former home.

### R7. Ordinary days matter

**Read:** **#24 / Mother's Rosario** on the
[official Mother's Rosario episode 24 page](https://www.swordart-onlineusa.com/mothers_rosario/story/?no=24).
Allow three minutes; stop before the final email introduces a new event.

**Teaching paraphrase:** The synopsis describes Yuuki joining cookouts and
quests with players in ALO, attending classes alongside Asuna and friends,
and taking part in trips to Kyoto. It gives everyday shared activities a
place beside extraordinary challenges. These activities provide concrete
examples to compare when asking what participation in a world can mean.

**Question to carry:** would counting successful logins tell us enough about
this participation? Identify something that such a measure would miss.

**Citation location:** R7, episode 24 synopsis, first three sentences.

## Terms for the discussion

- **Reported connection:** two events are linked in an account. That alone
  does not identify the mechanism connecting them.
- **Hypothesis:** an explanation to test. The Week 6 alternatives are
  classroom proposals, not additional events in the story.
- **Participation:** having an opportunity to do something that matters to
  you, with a meaningful role in deciding how it happens.
- **Support map:** our classroom diagram linking a person's goal, required
  resources, other people's contributions and unresolved needs.
