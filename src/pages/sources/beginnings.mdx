## What these documents are

These official English websites promote television episodes and films. Their
synopses select events and perspectives for prospective audiences. We use
them to identify those selections, not as independent eyewitness testimony.
The television continuity is our main reference; *Progressive* revisits early
Aincrad through another adaptation. Name which account you use.

The pages supply no publication dates. Cite the website, episode or film,
heading, URL and your access date; use "n.d." where a date is needed. Dates
inside their stories belong to the event chronology, not the publication
history. Our 2035 seminar dates are a separate teaching timetable.

## Week 1: the promise of Full-Dive

Read [Source A in the Aincrad packet](/sources/aincrad/#a-the-launch-announcement),
then its linked official episode 1 synopsis. That card owns our paraphrase
of the opening: use it rather than adding facts from memory. Pair it with the
[opening notes](/lectures/week-01/) to distinguish what access to a virtual
world offers from who controls its conditions.

**Useful terms:** a *platform* is the organised technical environment within
which people act; *access* is being able to participate; *control* concerns
who can change or refuse the conditions of participation. These are our
analytical terms, not additional claims about the device's engineering.

## Week 3: craft and meals

### B1. Lisbeth's commission

**Read:** the full short synopsis under **#07 / The Temperature of the Heart**
on the [official Aincrad episode 7 page](https://www.swordart-onlineusa.com/aincrad/story/?no=07).
Allow three minutes.

**Teaching paraphrase:** Kirito commissions a sword from Lisbeth, a
blacksmith, and presents a rare weapon as his standard. When he tests her
best existing sword, it breaks. Lisbeth says she could make a satisfactory
replacement if she obtained a rare metal. The commission depends on both
specialised work and access to material.

**Use it for:** tracing dependencies. The last sentence identifies the
relationship we will examine; the synopsis does not give a complete market
system, production process or fair price.

**Citation location:** B1, episode 7 synopsis, sentences about the durability
test and Lisbeth's reply.

### B2. A meal that requires more than an ingredient

**Read:** the full short synopsis under **#08 / The Sword Dance of Black and
White** on the [official Aincrad episode 8 page](https://www.swordart-onlineusa.com/aincrad/story/?no=08).
Allow three minutes.

**Teaching paraphrase:** Kirito obtains rare food but lacks the cooking skill
to use it. While trying to sell it, he meets Asuna, whose cooking skill is
advanced. She agrees to prepare it in exchange for half, and they need her
utensils. The useful result depends on an ingredient, learned skill,
equipment and an agreement.

**Use it for:** asking what makes cooperation attractive and whose
contribution a story of "the person who found the food" might overlook.

**Citation location:** B2, episode 8 synopsis, the final three sentences.
The opening supplies its place in the story's chronology.

**Useful terms:** a *dependency* is something an activity needs from another
person or resource. An *exchange* is an agreed transfer; we can analyse its
terms without assuming an entire economic system from one example.

## Week 4: perspective and shared information

### B3. Why revisit the beginning?

**Read:** on the [official Progressive introduction](https://saop-anime.com/intro-character/),
read the paragraphs under **Introduction** beginning **"Sword Art Online
Progressive"** and **"2021 saw"**. Allow three minutes; the sales and release
promotion are not required.

**Teaching paraphrase:** The introduction presents Progressive as a return
to the beginnings of Aincrad, starting on its first floor. It describes
Aria of a Starless Night as retelling the early period from Asuna's point
of view rather than Kirito's. The announced change is therefore one of
perspective as well as expanded attention to the opening.

**Use it for:** identifying an adaptation's stated purpose before comparing
its story with a television synopsis. Revisit [Source C](/sources/aincrad/#c-revisiting-the-opening-through-asuna)
for the short *Aria* press-release synopsis used in the first assessment.

**Citation location:** B3, *Scherzo of Deep Night* website, Introduction,
Progressive and *Aria* paragraphs.

### B4. Information within a divided group

**Read:** the short **Story** text on the
[official Scherzo of Deep Night story page](https://saop-anime.com/intro-character/story/).
Allow four minutes.

**Teaching paraphrase:** After clearing the first floor, Asuna continues with
Kirito. Argo's work as an information broker supports their progress.
Meanwhile, two leading guilds, the Aincrad Liberation Squad and Dragon Knights
Brigade, come into conflict despite needing to cooperate. The synopsis also
hints at someone influencing that conflict from behind the scenes.

**Use it for:** asking who obtains, shares and acts on information. The
classroom charter is our proposed response to that problem; it is not a rule
reported by the film synopsis.

**Citation location:** B4, Story, the paragraphs about Argo and the two guilds.

**Useful terms:** a *guild* is a player group; an *information broker* gathers
or supplies information that others can use. The source establishes Argo's
role, but does not give us a complete account of her methods or arrangements.
