## How these accounts were prepared

*After Aincrad* uses an imagined 2035 classroom to study selected *Sword Art
Online* events as history. The people and events belong to that fictional
setting; the works and publication information credited below belong to the
actual source record. This is the course's educational framing, not a claim
that SAO happened in our world.

The **After Aincrad course team** prepared the assigned accounts in original
wording from official English synopses, introductions, profiles and press
releases. They are teaching accounts, not recovered diaries, interviews or
independent survivor testimony. We have not invented quotations, archival
publication dates or primary records. Paragraph numbers identify the course's
own text; use them when citing the assigned reading.

Every required account is complete on this site. The links below allow checking
and further reading without requiring students to buy, watch or retrieve other
material. Most underlying pages are promotional summaries: useful for locating
what a work presents, but not independent eyewitness or technical evidence.
Agreement between accounts sharing these materials is not separate corroboration.

  activities, ranking system and player-versus-player advantage.

The webpage is undated. Its promotional description of safety is not an independent
safety evaluation. It does not establish a memory-extraction mechanism, a
particular public-square incident or an accessibility policy. The Week 9 shared-place
scenario and interface response are classroom proposals.

## Briefing and chronology

The [beginner briefing](/start/) and [event chronology](/sources/#event-chronology)
condense the accounts above. Their additional date/ending check is official USA
[Aincrad episode 14, “The End of the World”](https://www.swordart-onlineusa.com/aincrad/story/?no=14):
the opening places the 75th-floor battle in **November 2024**. Episode 2 places
the first month in **December 2022**; the Fairy Dance introduction then establishes
return after the battle with Kayaba/Heathcliff. **Approximately two years** is a
synthesis of these points, not an independently quoted duration or an exact ending day.
We do not infer that players completed every one of the 100 floors before returning.

The **2026** Augma date is explicit in the *Ordinal Scale* Prologue. GGO's account
gives **one year after SAO was cleared**. The selected Yuuki and Underworld extracts
do not provide complete calendar years, so their rows use relative periods.
The 2035 seminar dates are course-design dates, not dates assigned to SAO events.

## Citation and access notes

This source register was reviewed on **28 September 2026**.
Use **n.d.** for an undated webpage; a copyright footer is not a publication date.
If you choose to quote an external original, quote its own wording accurately and
identify the original location. For required coursework, citing the assigned
course account and paragraph is sufficient; see the
[citation guide](/sources/#use-and-credit-a-source).

The [optional methods](/sources/methods/) keep their real authors and original
references on their own page. They provide analytical tools, not further SAO
historical evidence. Missing original material should remain a named limitation;
we do not fill it by inventing records, testimony or remembered plot detail.
