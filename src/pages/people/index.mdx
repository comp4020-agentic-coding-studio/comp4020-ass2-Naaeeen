## Bring a question

A source passage, a rough claim or an unfinished caption is enough for a
consultation. Ask during the seminar or at its start or end to arrange time.
For something private, request an individual conversation; you do not need to
explain it in front of the class.

[Policies and support](/policies/) covers preparation, participation, extensions
and accessible ways to work with the material.
