## This week's question

**Who can take part when the boundaries of everyday life change?**

Mother's Rosario gives us a different reason to value a virtual world.
This week begins with what Yuuki wants to do and who she wants to do it
with. We then ask which parts of participation depend on a device, which
on other people's work, and which on being included in a shared activity.

## Prepare in 25 minutes

Spend ten minutes on [R5-R7](/sources/returns/#week-7-a-world-worth-participating-in)
and their free English episode synopses. Read the
[participation notes](/lectures/week-07/) for eight minutes. In the final
seven, choose one goal named in the packet and sketch the steps needed to
participate. Mark the people, resources and decisions the source identifies;
leave unreported arrangements as questions.

## In the seminar: 90 minutes

- **0-15:** compare the goals you chose. Explain what would count as a
  worthwhile experience from the participant's point of view.
- **15-35:** build a participation journey with four stages: express a
  wish, arrange access, join the activity, and respond afterwards. Attach
  source evidence where available; label the rest as questions.
- **35-45:** trace who contributes at each stage. Identify one part a
  device alone cannot provide, and one assumption about other people's
  availability that the account leaves open.
- **45-50:** take a break.
- **50-70:** test a **classroom scenario** in which one helpful person is
  unavailable. This interruption is invented. Propose a different arrangement
  and show where the participant could accept, reject or change it.
- **70-85:** exchange journeys. Check whether the revision supports the
  chosen goal or quietly replaces it with what helpers find convenient.
  Use a second case from R5-R7 to test that judgement.
- **85-90:** add a 100-word explanation of the most consequential change
  to your journey and one question the evidence cannot answer.

## Take away

Keep the journey and explanation. They help distinguish technical access
from participation in the [incident study](/assessments/incident-study/).
For [Week 8](/sessions/08-why-return/), choose a contrasting earlier case
and consider whether the same reason for valuing a world explains both.
