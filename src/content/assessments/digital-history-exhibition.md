**Draft outline. Worth 50%. Due Friday 11 May 2035 at 5 pm, Canberra time.**

Curate a small exhibition answering the course question: **why do people
continue to enter, inhabit and protect virtual worlds after the SAO incident?**
Make the sequence of items develop an argument, with captions that help a
first-time visitor understand both the evidence and your interpretation.

The planned format is a slide presentation or simple webpage, accompanied by
a source list and a short curatorial statement. Programming is not required.
The final item count, scope, accessibility requirements and marking criteria
will be developed with the later teaching weeks. This outline is not yet a
complete submission brief.

Your [incident study](/assessments/incident-study/) can supply one carefully
researched part of the exhibition. The final argument should also make
connections across the course rather than treating one incident as the whole
history.
