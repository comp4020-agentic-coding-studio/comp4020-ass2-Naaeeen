Ren supports the weekly source activities and peer discussions. Bring a
passage you cannot locate, two accounts that seem to disagree, or a paragraph
where the evidence and your inference have become difficult to separate.
Together you can identify a smaller question and a next step.

Use seminar work time for a quick check of a timeline, source ledger or
exhibition caption. If the question needs longer, ask at the end of class to
arrange a consultation. You can share a written question or work with a
partner instead of speaking to the whole group.

For extensions, changes to an assessment plan or broader access arrangements,
speak with [Mara Vale](/people/mara-vale/). Start with the
[course policies](/policies/) if you are unsure which kind of help to request.
