Before building After Aincrad, I asked for research into how to use coding agents effectively. I reused my Crit 5 rules, which Claude adapted during setup ([0b50b24](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Naaeeen/commit/0b50b24)). Codex examined OpenAI and Anthropic guidance, studies of instruction files and practical workflows, then tested revised instructions against the existing version. The comparison used 18 scored runs across nine pairs, with fresh workspaces, fixed source files and dependencies, and a separate, unchanged marking script. This turned recommendations such as reading only relevant context, planning uncertain changes and requesting independent review into practices that could be examined in this project ([2b885c8](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Naaeeen/commit/2b885c8c9529b744c0c5b4dace351fa5299e72a6)).

All 18 final versions passed the automatic checks, but a separate review found something those checks missed. Text that merely looked like a link could satisfy one check without there being any clickable link. The main agent reproduced the finding before changing the website's check to inspect actual links. It then passed 31 cases covering valid and invalid examples ([785b1ce](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Naaeeen/commit/785b1ce2b4d7fd1308221b8712676804a5a23dca)). Passing tests was therefore only one part of the review.

I also asked for decisions and research to be kept current throughout development. I recommend three documents to the tutor: [implementation goals](../../planning/implementation-goals.md) sets out the five points for my review and what each stage must demonstrate; [implementation research](../../IMPLEMENTATION-RESEARCH.md) connects sources and alternatives to decisions; and [process evidence](../../PROCESS-EVIDENCE.md) connects observed problems to changes, commits and checks. The [c14d959](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Naaeeen/commit/c14d9590f2edf1fc4c3d55173c92a0e08049bdf0) and [c14d959...8d518f9](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Naaeeen/compare/c14d9590f2edf1fc4c3d55173c92a0e08049bdf0...8d518f9c58e5ae20c9e827af0e5772633c5cb810) show these records developing with the work. CLAUDE.md kept standing instructions, while PLAN.md recorded the current position and next action. These documents gave later tasks the decisions they needed to continue.

Sword Art Online first interested me in computing. I wanted After Aincrad to teach its events as history from 2035, and insisted that complete newcomers should understand it. Review found that Week 2 instead taught the franchise's publication and promotional history. The correction began with the plan and instructions, then changed the readings, source accounts, activities, slides and assessment together. The instructions now require unfamiliar terms to be introduced before students analyse them, and preparation to lead to a seminar output and assessment ([1c27051...9741b89](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Naaeeen/compare/1c27051bc85009e128952a9afff34729670ddc04...9741b8908915bac9fda81faf8aad5d7cdde1a7dd)).

I later pointed out that the website had too many categories to make the starting point clear, while important pages such as the Atlas still needed direct access. The revision added “Start here”, a weekly itinerary and a resource desk. Teaching copy, source packets, interface work and slides were assigned to separate agents with defined responsibilities. The main agent checked their findings against files, sources and browser observations. Browser checks also found the phone week selector below the long introduction and slide controls clipped despite a successful build. The selector moved into the first screen, the slides were corrected, and CLAUDE.md gained checks for both problems. All ten slides were examined in embedded and standalone views at desktop and phone sizes ([7625ae9](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Naaeeen/commit/7625ae94b8f8207e2432c643275bcb0f1468a0c8)). My review gates remain the place to judge whether the historical voice, course progression and presentation work together.
