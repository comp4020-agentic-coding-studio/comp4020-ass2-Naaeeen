Sword Art Online first made me interested in computing. For After Aincrad, I wanted a course set in 2035 that teaches its events as history. I also insisted that someone unfamiliar with the series should understand it. That meant explaining unfamiliar terms before asking students to analyse them, and making it clear what to read, what to do in class, and how that work leads into assessment.

Before implementing the course, I asked for research into agent best practices. The agent examined OpenAI and Anthropic guidance, studies of instruction files, and practitioner workflows, then compared the original instructions with two revisions. Across nine pairs, there were 18 scored runs in fresh workspaces, with source files, dependencies and configuration held fixed. The grading rules were kept outside those workspaces. Every final result passed the mechanical checks, but independent review still found a problem ([research and comparison](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Naaeeen/commit/2b885c8)).

The first useful discovery was that the checker could mistake text that looked like a link for an actual clickable link. A reviewer spotted this, and the main agent reproduced it before accepting the finding. The correction reads the HTML's real links instead of searching for a matching piece of text. It passed 31 cases covering valid and invalid examples ([fix](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Naaeeen/commit/785b1ce)). This made independent review concrete: passing tests was a reason to inspect what they proved, not a reason to stop looking. The revised instructions also called for reading relevant files, planning uncertain changes, assigning bounded tasks, and rerunning checks affected by a correction.

I asked for five stages where I could review the work, with research and documentation maintained as decisions changed. Teaching copy, source packets, interface work and slides were assigned separately. The main agent checked reviewers' claims against files, sources and browser observations. Standing rules stayed in CLAUDE.md; PLAN.md held the current next step. For the tutor, I recommend [implementation goals](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Naaeeen/blob/dea0fe55e488d902895041d4210dd867cfcbbbf3/docs/planning/implementation-goals.md) to follow the five review gates and acceptance criteria, [implementation research](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Naaeeen/blob/dea0fe55e488d902895041d4210dd867cfcbbbf3/docs/IMPLEMENTATION-RESEARCH.md) to see which sources and alternatives informed decisions, and [process evidence](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Naaeeen/blob/dea0fe55e488d902895041d4210dd867cfcbbbf3/docs/PROCESS-EVIDENCE.md) to trace problems through changes, commits and checks. These were working documents, [created](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Naaeeen/commit/c14d959) and [updated during implementation](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Naaeeen/compare/c14d959...8d518f9).

The second discovery concerned the student's first action. I pointed out that the website had too many categories to show where to begin, while important pages such as Atlas still needed direct access. The revised course added “Start here”, an ordered weekly itinerary and a resource desk. Six new checks covered concrete relationships, including preparation order and Week 9's reuse of a Week 2 note ([implementation](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Naaeeen/commit/9741b89)). This connected the navigation to the teaching plan instead of simply adding more labels.

Browser inspection then found that the phone's week selector sat below a long introduction, outside the first screen. The agent moved it into the header and checked the result. CLAUDE.md gained a requirement to inspect the first action and week selector at phone size ([review and refinement](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Naaeeen/commit/7625ae9)). Automated checks established the written relationships; browser checks established where controls appeared. My review gates remained the place to judge whether the historical voice and course made sense for a newcomer.
