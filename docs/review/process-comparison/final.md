# Process overview

After Aincrad is a history course set in 2035, based on Sword Art Online. I wanted students new to SAO to understand what happened, know what to read first and see what each week asked of them. Before building those pages, I asked Codex to research how it should work. It read OpenAI and Anthropic guidance, studies of instruction files and practitioner examples. I started from my Crit 5 rules, which Claude had adapted to the course-site starter ([0b50b24](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Naaeeen/commit/0b50b24)).

I asked the agent to try the old and revised rules on the same small jobs in fresh copies of the project. The code used to judge the results stayed outside those copies. All 18 scored runs passed the automatic checks. A separate review agent then found a revealing mistake: one checker accepted text that looked like a link, even though there was no clickable link. The main agent reproduced the problem before replacing that check. The replacement reads real HTML links and passed 31 valid and invalid cases. This gave independent review a specific job: check whether the tests were asking the right question ([2b885c8](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Naaeeen/commit/2b885c8), [785b1ce](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Naaeeen/commit/785b1ce)).

I carried three practices into implementation: check the brief and original documentation before uncertain decisions; make a short plan before changing several parts of the site; and give agents separate files to work on, then have the main agent verify their results. I also asked them to keep the decisions written down.

The [implementation goals](../../planning/implementation-goals.md) say what must be ready at each of my five review points. The [research log](../../IMPLEMENTATION-RESEARCH.md) records the sources, options and reasons for each choice. The [evidence bank](../../PROCESS-EVIDENCE.md) links important problems to their fixes and commits. These are the three documents I recommend to the tutor. Keeping them current meant the next task could start from decisions already made. CLAUDE.md keeps the rules for future work; PLAN.md says where we are and what comes next ([c14d959](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Naaeeen/commit/c14d959), [c14d959...8d518f9](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Naaeeen/compare/c14d959...8d518f9)).

We built one complete teaching week before extending the course ([4fc9cde](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Naaeeen/commit/4fc9cde)). Later, I clarified that SAO should be taught as events that happened in the course's world. Review found that Week 2 still asked students to compare publicity and release dates. The correction started with a revised plan and CLAUDE.md rules, then changed the readings, activity, slides and assessment together. Students now compare accounts of the events and cite particular paragraphs; a separate credits page identifies the works behind them. CLAUDE.md now requires each week to connect its preparation and classroom activity to an assessment ([1c27051...9741b89](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Naaeeen/compare/1c27051...9741b89)).

I also challenged the crowded navigation. Students needed a clear starting point while Atlas and other useful pages stayed easy to find. The revision added a weekly route alongside direct links to resources. Browser checks then found the phone week selector below a long introduction. The agent moved it into the first screen and added a CLAUDE.md rule to check that starting actions are visible on a phone. Tests check details such as reading order, preparation totals and the Week 9 link to Week 2 notes. I kept the judgement of voice, appeal and whether the course hangs together for my review ([9741b89](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Naaeeen/commit/9741b89), [7625ae9](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Naaeeen/commit/7625ae9)).
