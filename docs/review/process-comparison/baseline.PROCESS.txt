# Process overview

Before building course content, I asked Codex to research how the agent itself should work. I wanted After Aincrad to treat SAO as history in 2035, with a clear route for students who knew nothing about it. I carried forward my Crit 5 harness, adapted during Claude's setup, and asked the agent to test revisions before using them ([0b50b24](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Naaeeen/commit/0b50b24), [c14d959](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Naaeeen/commit/c14d959)).

The research covered OpenAI and Anthropic guidance, studies of instruction files, and practitioner workflows. I asked for recommendations to be tested against our repository. Two rounds of paired comparisons produced 18 scored runs, using fresh fixtures, fixed starting code and configuration, and a frozen grader kept outside the trial workspaces. All 18 scored runs passed the mechanical checks. An independent agent reviewed masked outputs and found that one generated regex accepted href text inside another HTML attribute as a real link. The integrating agent reproduced the failure, and the eventual parser-based replacement passed 31 valid/invalid cases. That finding made checking the evaluator itself part of acceptance. The adopted harness clarified when to plan, what context to load, and when verification was sufficient; it also removed redundant build cycles ([2b885c8](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Naaeeen/commit/2b885c8), [785b1ce](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Naaeeen/commit/785b1ce)).

I required research to continue when a source, design choice or failure needed investigation. For a closer view of how I directed the work, I recommend three documents: the [implementation goals](docs/planning/implementation-goals.md) define five human review gates and their acceptance criteria; the [implementation research](docs/IMPLEMENTATION-RESEARCH.md), especially its evaluator probe and Gate 4 decisions, connects sources and alternatives to design choices; and the [process evidence bank](docs/PROCESS-EVIDENCE.md) preserves problems, corrections and commit evidence. I required the agent to maintain them as decisions changed. CLAUDE.md holds the reusable rules, while PLAN.md carries the current state and next action. Each new stage could recover the requirements, the reason for a choice and the evidence for accepting it ([c14d959](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Naaeeen/commit/c14d959), [c14d959...8d518f9](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Naaeeen/compare/c14d959...8d518f9)).

The workflow became research, plan, implement a representative unit, inspect it, then extend it after my review. Agents received separate responsibility for teaching copy, sources, UI and slides; the integrating agent checked their findings against the files, original sources and browser output. When I clarified the historical viewpoint, review found that Week 2 still compared publicity and release dates. The revised plan changed the readings, activity and assessment together: students now work with case accounts and paragraph references, while linked credits explain the underlying works. CLAUDE.md consequently requires unfamiliar terms to be introduced and preparation to connect to a seminar output and assessment ([1c27051...9741b89](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Naaeeen/compare/1c27051...9741b89)).

My navigation feedback tested whether that course was usable. Students needed an obvious beginning while Atlas and other important resources stayed visible. The result combines a weekly route with direct resource access. Browser inspection found the phone week selector buried below the introductory explanation, and embedded slide controls outside their frame. The agent corrected both and checked the actual layouts at 1920×1080 and 390×844. Those findings became explicit CLAUDE.md checks for first actions and embedded slides. Tests now protect reading-order consistency, preparation totals and cross-week links; I reserved historical voice, visual appeal and course coherence for human review ([9741b89](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Naaeeen/commit/9741b89), [7625ae9](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Naaeeen/commit/7625ae9)).
